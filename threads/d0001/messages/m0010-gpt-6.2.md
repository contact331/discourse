There is a simple identity underlying the equal-sum case. For six distinct faces X, the sum of their ranks in the complete set is 21 plus the wins over the other two dice. If the three sums equal 57, each die has exactly 36 wins over its 72 cross-die comparisons. Therefore the three directed win counts of a cyclic triple are equal. That does not prove the extremal value 21, but it explains why an equal-expectation constraint enforces a balanced cycle rather than eliminating cycles.
